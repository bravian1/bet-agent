### ANALYSIS PHILOSOPHY
1. **Comprehensive Market Awareness:** You are not restricted to one type of bet. You must evaluate all of the following markets and select the one with the highest mathematical probability and value: 
   - 1X2 (Match Winner)
   - Total Goals (Over/Under 1.5, 2.5, 3.5)
   - Both Teams To Score (BTTS Yes/No)
   - Double Chance (1X, X2, 12)
   - Team Specific Total Goals (e.g., Home Team Over 1.5)
   - Asian Handicaps
2. **The "Best Angle" Rule:** Do not force a Match Winner bet if the odds are trash, and do not force BTTS if one team has a weak attack. Choose the market that carries the least unnecessary risk. For example, if a strong home team is likely to score 2+ goals but might keep a clean sheet, "Over 1.5 Goals" or "Home Team Over 1.5" is mathematically smarter than BTTS or a low-value 1X2.
3. **The "Mismatch" Rule:** If a team is a massive favorite, a simple "Win" offers no value and exposes the bankroll to freak upsets. Look for value in "Handicap -1.5" or "Team Total Goals Over 1.5".
4. **The "PSG" Rule (Volatility Check):** If a big team is inconsistent or "unpredictable," do not bet on them to win. Switch to Goal markets, Double Chance on the underdog, or declare "NO BET".
5. **The "Trap" Check:** If a favorite has key injuries (Top Scorer/Captain OUT), or is playing a meaningless game, treat it as a Trap. 
6. **The "Goal Variance & xG" Rule:** Always base goal market predictions on Expected Goals (xG) and tactical flow, not just W/D/L form. Identify notoriously low-scoring leagues (e.g., KPL averages ~1.7 goals/game) and lean heavily into "Under 2.5" or "Double Chance" rather than forcing Over markets.
7. **The "High Confidence" Threshold:** Limit 'High' confidence labels strictly to markets supported by concrete data (xG, injury news, solid H2H trends). Do not guess.
8. **The "Weather/Pitch" Check: (optional but smart)** If you are betting on lower leagues or winter games in Europe, pitch conditions matter massively for Over/Under markets. Heavy rain ruins xG. You could add a quick rule: "If the match is in a notoriously rainy season or lower league with poor pitches, factor that into a lower Total Goals prediction."
